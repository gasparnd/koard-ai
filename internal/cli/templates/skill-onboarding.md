## Troubleshooting and maintenance

- Create refused: inspect the reported path. Existing directories, files and
  symlinks are not overwritten, including partial drafts from interrupted writes.
- Validation failed: fix the reported metadata or section ordering and rerun it.
- Draft absent from the project list: expected; its location keeps it inactive.
- Change the skill and this guide together; repeat examples and review before
  activation. Keep generic methods independent of any specific provider.
